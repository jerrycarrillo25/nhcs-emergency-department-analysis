# Emergency Department Utilization Analysis

## Project Overview

This healthcare analytics portfolio project examines 2022 emergency department (ED) utilization data from the National Hospital Care Survey (NHCS).

Using SQL in Google BigQuery, I cleaned and validated the data and developed analytical views to investigate encounter volume, patient demographics, selected health conditions, and discharge outcomes. Tableau Public is used to visualize the findings and explore their potential implications for emergency department staffing and operational planning.

## Business Question

How do ED patient demographics, health conditions, discharge outcomes, and encounter volume vary throughout 2022, and what do these patterns suggest about staffing and operational planning?

## Tools

- **SQL:** Data cleaning, validation, aggregation, and analytical views
- **Google BigQuery:** Data storage and analysis
- **Python and Pandas:** Data format conversion and initial inspection
- **Tableau Public:** Data visualization and dashboard development

## Data Source

National Center for Health Statistics (NCHS), *2022 National Hospital Care Survey (NHCS) public-use data files.*

[View the NHCS data and documentation](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/nhcs/data/index.html#cdc_data_description_data_use_and_impact-2022)

**Note:** This dataset represents a sample of ED encounters from multiple hospitals, not the complete records of one actual hospital. The project uses the data as a simulated hospital operations case study.
